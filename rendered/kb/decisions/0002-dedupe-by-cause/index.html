0002 · Deduplicate by cause and evidence

owner keyroll-99 accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Scope: this exported KB and its triage guidance. Replaces: decision 0001, preserved as accepted history. This record does not claim the course’s live desk or production software has been changed.

Context

The review of #4471 shows distinct mechanisms behind matching subjects. Browser-derived intake labels also disagree with tenant-specific classification. A merge can conceal a different defect and leave it without an engineering ticket.

Decision

Deduplicate by supported cause, not subject equality. Join the tenant directory and compare channel, form version and locale using the supplied causeOf/sameCause functions. Read report bodies before merging to retain contradictory or unexplained evidence. Keep one engineering issue per established cause, with counts based on distinct report IDs.

Consequences

  • Same subject with different causes stays in separate tickets.
  • Different subjects with the same supported mechanism can share a ticket.
  • Unknown tenants and unexplained symptoms need review; “no cause” is not a matching cause.
  • New reports or tenant changes require recomputing counts and rebuilding the KB.
  • This needs more desk attention than subject-only matching, but avoids hiding a second fix.
