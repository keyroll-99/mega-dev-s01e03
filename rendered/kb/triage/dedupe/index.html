Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

The rule

A report is a duplicate when evidence supports the same cause, under decision 0002. This replaces the subject-only rule in historical decision 0001.

  1. Deduplicate overlapping export rows by report ID.
  2. Look up the tenant’s locale and booking form, and check the report channel.
  3. Use causeOf and compare mechanisms with sameCause; inspect the body for contradictions.
  4. Merge only supported same-cause reports. Different or unknown causes stay apart.
  5. Keep engineering tickets, computed counts and the rendered KB current as reports arrive.
Subjects help find reports; they do not establish duplicate causes. Intake tags and browser locales are guesses. See the [review of #4471](/kb/incidents/4471-review).

When you are unsure

Keep it apart for review. Record what is missing or contradictory. Do not turn uncertainty into a confirmed root cause or hide it in a known ticket.
